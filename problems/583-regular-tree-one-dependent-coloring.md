# 583. Automorphism-invariant one-dependent colorings of regular trees

**Area:** Applied probability and distributed graph coloring

**Status:** ✅ SOLVED

**Last checked:** 2026-10-13

## Problem statement

Let $`T_d=(V,E)`$ be the infinite connected $`d`$-regular tree, where $`d\ge3`$. Does there exist a finite integer $`q=q(d)`$ and a random field $`X\in\{1,\ldots,q\}^{V}`$ with all of the following properties?

1. Almost surely $`X_u\ne X_v`$ for every edge $`uv\in E`$.
2. For any two finite vertex sets $`A,B`$ at graph distance greater than one, the vectors $`X|_A`$ and $`X|_B`$ are independent.
3. The law of $`X`$ is invariant under every graph automorphism of $`T_d`$ and under every permutation of the $`q`$ color names.

Settle existence for every $`d\ge3`$, or find a degree for which it is impossible for all finite $`q`$. There is no requirement that the field be a finite-range function of independent vertex labels or that it be generated by a bounded-round algorithm.

## Application

Proper random colors model conflict-free assignments on networks. One-dependence asks that separated regions have exactly independent assignments, while the symmetry requirements prevent use of a distinguished root or favored color. This tests the limits of spatially local random dependence beyond specific implementations by distributed algorithms.
