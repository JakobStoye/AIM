Author: Kaiwen Jin. Contact: yui.ui.siki@gmail.com.

verification script, and drafted the manuscript. Two separate fresh-context
AI reviewers examined the same anonymous draft, one for reader comprehension
and one for mathematical objections and the cited sources. Their comments
informed one revision clarifying notation, the supplementary script and
source-version locators. The mathematical construction and calculation were
unchanged. The reviewers did not review the final revised bytes.

contexts and disclosure instructions were used; physical filesystem isolation

review, a formal proof certificate, a novelty certificate or acceptance by the
