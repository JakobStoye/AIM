# Manuscript

`main.tex` is a standalone English manuscript, authored by Kaiwen Jin
(`yui.ui.siki@gmail.com`). `main.pdf` is the compiled eight-page artifact.
The full published argument is in the manuscript and its public references;
no private supplement is required. The final section discloses AI assistance.

Build with `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex`.
The source uses standard LaTeX packages and contains its own bibliography.
Numerical tables are checked against `../numerical_case/summaries/`.

Theorem 1.1 has the exact joint-kernel quantifiers of AIM 519. Polynomial
potentials suffice. Supporting finite computations are confined to Appendix
B and are not premises of the proof. The manuscript presents a solution
claim for independent mathematical review.
