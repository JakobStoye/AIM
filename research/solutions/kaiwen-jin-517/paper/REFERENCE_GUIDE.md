Guermond's exact archived chapter files supply the standard tools:

- [Chapter 1](https://people.tamu.edu/~guermond/M661_FALL_2025/chap01.pdf):
  Theorem 1.47 (Fubini).
- [Chapter 2](https://people.tamu.edu/~guermond/M661_FALL_2025/chap02.pdf):
  Proposition 2.9 (completeness), Theorem 2.2 (Lebesgue differentiation),
  Theorem 2.21 (density), Corollary 2.25 (weak coordinate rule),
  Theorems 2.30 (extension) and 2.35 (Rellich).
- [Chapter 3](https://people.tamu.edu/~guermond/M661_FALL_2025/chap03.pdf):
  Theorem 3.10 (H1 trace and zero-trace kernel),
  Lemmas 3.24 and 3.27 (mean and zero-trace Poincare).
- [Appendix A](https://people.tamu.edu/~guermond/M661_FALL_2025/App_A_and_B_and_ref.pdf):
  Theorem A.16 (Riesz representation).

Harmonic-extension formulas, matrix comparisons, determinant bounds, the
energy-ball spectrum and the cube transfer are proved in the manuscript.
Li–Wang and Bandtlow–Chu are background, not sources already proving
the measurable-coefficient cutoff theorem. No resolution for all PDEs
in Ma's framework is asserted.
