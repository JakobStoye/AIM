# AI-use and review disclosure

Author: Kaiwen Jin. Contact: yui.ui.siki@gmail.com.

OpenAI Codex provided substantial assistance with mathematical exploration, proof drafting, source checking and manuscript preparation. Two fresh AI contexts separately supplied a reader evaluation and a referee-style evaluation of the same frozen draft, without the other evaluator's report or an author defense. The manuscript was revised after that feedback.

These AI evaluations do not constitute independent human review or formal verification. No separate detailed AI review report is published with this package. The manuscript contains the complete analytic argument, and independent mathematical assessment is requested.
