# Comparison with AIM problem 630

Target: [Relative density of sets generated by fixed-parameter extrapolation](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/a153f469ee0ea64e876b2ad97c79d252c19a3e22/problems/630-extrapolation-relative-density.md), revision `a153f469ee0ea64e876b2ad97c79d252c19a3e22`.

Submitted result: Theorem 1 of [*Relative density under fixed-parameter extrapolation*](paper/relative_density.pdf), by Kaiwen Jin, October 14, 2026. This is a complete affirmative solution claim, subject to mathematical review.

| Requirement of the frozen problem | Manuscript location and coverage |
|---|---|
| The smallest set containing 0 and 1 and closed under $`(1-\lambda)a+\lambda b`$ | Section 1, p. 1, defines precisely this $`Q_\lambda`$. |
| No topological closure | Section 1 explicitly restricts generation to finitely many operations. Equation (1) imports membership in that algebraic generated set. The main proof, p. 3, uses only finite digit polynomials. |
| Every real $`\lambda\notin[0,1]`$, ambient space $`\mathbb R`$ | Theorem 1, p. 1. Lemma 2 applies at every permitted parameter; Lemma 3 uses $`\mathbb Z`$ for the real case; the proof on p. 3 preserves the real ambient space. |
| Every nonreal $`\lambda`$, ambient space $`\mathbb C`$ | Theorem 1, p. 1. Lemma 3 uses the full-rank real lattice $`\mathbb Z+\lambda\mathbb Z`$; the proof on p. 3 applies without an algebraicity assumption. |
| A finite radius depending only on $`\lambda`$, followed by every target point $`z`$ | Lemma 3 supplies a finite covering radius $`\rho`$. The main proof fixes all choices before $`z`$, then gives $`R_\lambda=\lvert\lambda(1-\lambda)\rvert^k\rho`$, p. 3. |
| An actual $`q\in Q_\lambda`$ with $`\lvert z-q\rvert\le R_\lambda`$ | Equation (6) and the final paragraph, p. 3: choose a finite expansion $`v`$ and set $`q=c(\ell+v)`$. The cited polynomial theorem gives its membership. |
| Characterize parameters if the universal assertion fails | The submitted theorem asserts the universal statement on both requested domains, so its conditional failure branch does not arise. |

The proof uses the published integer-polynomial characterization of Fenner, Green and Homer, [DOI 10.1007/s00454-025-00816-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-025-00816-4), Fact 3.3 (p. 10), Theorem 3.6 and Corollary 3.8 (p. 11). Their Lemma 6.19 and following remark (p. 40) are credited for the bounded-polynomial embedding idea. Their Open Question 11.3 (p. 61) is the question addressed here. The supplementary construction supplies an expanding polynomial, finite-digit covering and a common affine scale for all expansions.

This submission concerns bounded holes. It makes no claim that all these sets are uniformly discrete or that the separate discreteness conjecture is resolved. A bounded literature search and AI review do not establish priority or upstream acceptance.
