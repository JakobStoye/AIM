Original statement: [immutable repository revision a153f469ee0ea64e876b2ad97c79d252c19a3e22](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/a153f469ee0ea64e876b2ad97c79d252c19a3e22/problems/565-macbeath-center-asymmetry.md).
