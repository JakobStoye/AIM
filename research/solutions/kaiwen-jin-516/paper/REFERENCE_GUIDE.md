# Primary references and the uses made of them

1. S. A. Andersson, D. Madigan and M. D. Perlman, *A characterization of Markov
   equivalence classes for acyclic digraphs*, Annals of Statistics 25(2),
   505--541 (1997), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1031833662).
   The accessible [primary-author Technical Report 287 (1995)](https://stat.uw.edu/sites/default/files/files/reports/1995/tr287.pdf)
   is the version used for the manuscript's locators: Theorem 2.1 for skeleton
   and immoralities; Propositions 4.1--4.2 and Theorem 4.1 on printed pages
   10--11 for chain components, perfect orientations and the absence of an
   induced arrow-undirected flag; Appendix A for the graph conventions and
   perfect orientations. Journal pagination is not substituted for report
   pagination. The common-parent consequence and factorial bound are proved
   in Lemma 2.1 of the submitted manuscript.
2. D. Schmid and A. Sly, *On the number and size of Markov equivalence classes
   of random directed acyclic graphs*, [arXiv:2209.04395v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.04395v2),
   revised September 28, 2024. Theorem 1.1 and its following paragraph on page 2
   give the exponent 1+1/20 and the near-two question. Section 2, Lemma 3.1 and
   Proposition 3.2 supply method lineage for tower weights and layer tails. The submitted paper proves its quantitative
   versions directly; it does not assume the desired stronger tail from that
   source.

Both references identify the same finite simple-DAG setting used in the paper.
The manuscript uses the structural essential-graph theorem as an external
dependency and proves every new probability estimate internally.
