Original statement: [AIM 577 at revision c9929805cece044705abaaebe1cebddac1ba11a6](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/c9929805cece044705abaaebe1cebddac1ba11a6/problems/577-repeated-gue-strong-freeness.md).

Submitted manuscript: Kaiwen Jin, *Strong convergence for a repeated GUE interaction on three tensor legs* ([PDF](paper/repeated_gue_three_legs.pdf), [source](paper/repeated_gue_three_legs.tex)). The proposed outcome is affirmative operator-norm convergence for the full stated model.

## Public theorem dependencies and added application

The general comparison and free convergence theorems are credited to their original authors. The manuscript adds the explicit shared-realization covariance calculation and the application through full matrix pencils and a probabilistic trace lower bound. It does not claim a new general Gaussian comparison theorem.

| Public source | Manuscript use and applicability |
| --- | --- |
| Bandeira–Boedihardjo–van Handel, [arXiv:2108.06312v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.06312v2), Theorem 2.1 | Section 3 applies spectral comparison to the whole shifted Gaussian pencil. The deterministic coefficient is retained, and the noise bounds are `σ ≤ ||a_1||+||a_2||` and `v^2 ≤ 2(||a_1||_HS^2+||a_2||_HS^2)/N`. This theorem does not require independent matrix entries. |
| Gao–Kunnawalkam Elayavalli, [arXiv:2604.14106v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14106v3), Corollary 1.4 and Examples 2.5(3), with Jekel–Lee–Nelson–Pi, [arXiv:2506.19940v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19940v3), Theorem 4.3 and Proposition 1.8 | Lemma 3.1 uses the trace-symmetric block completely positive covariance. Lemma 2.2 verifies every required deterministic covariance-expression limit; the limiting scalar base is generated by the identity. |
| Jekel–Lee–Nelson–Pi, Lemma 3.9 | Section 4 obtains expected word-trace convergence for the jointly Gaussian tuple. The joint entry-covariance norm is `2/N`, and the deterministic base is the identity. |
| Haagerup–Thorbjørnsen, [Annals of Mathematics 162 (2005), 711–775](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2005.162.711), Theorem 2.4; Proposition 4.1 and Corollary 4.2 | Proposition 3.3 uses the deterministic matrix-pencil criterion. Lemma 4.1 uses Gaussian Poincaré on the actual shared scalar coordinates, giving word-trace variance `O(N^-4)`. The independent-GUE strong-convergence theorem is not substituted for the repeated model. |

The complete analytic argument is in the manuscript. No numerical or formal-proof certificate is supplied. The submission is a complete solution claim for independent mathematical review; it does not assert catalogue acceptance.
