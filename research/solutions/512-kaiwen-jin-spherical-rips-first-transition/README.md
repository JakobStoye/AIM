# 512: The first Vietoris--Rips homotopy transition of round spheres

Author: Kaiwen Jin. Contact: yui.ui.siki@gmail.com.

This package submits a complete affirmative proof claim for [Problem 512](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/a153f469ee0ea64e876b2ad97c79d252c19a3e22/problems/512-spherical-rips-first-transition.md) at repository revision `a153f469ee0ea64e876b2ad97c79d252c19a3e22`.

Theorem 1.1 identifies the ordinary strict Vietoris--Rips complex of the unit geodesic sphere, for every integer n >= 3 and every scale in some interval immediately above the regular-simplex threshold, with the join of the sphere and its rotational regular-simplex quotient. Sections 3 and 4 give the endpoint attachment and the transfer to strict scales. The argument uses the cited primary results of Mémoli--Wang, Ebert--Randal-Williams, Ji--Meng and Corvellec.
