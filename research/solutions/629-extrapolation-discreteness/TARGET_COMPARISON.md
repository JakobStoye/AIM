The proof uses two public theorem interfaces from Fenner, Green and Homer:

- Reference [1], *Fixed-Parameter Extrapolation and Aperiodic Order*, [journal version](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-025-00816-4), Theorem 3.6 and Corollary 3.8, printed page 11: the characterization of generated integer polynomials and evaluation at a complex parameter. The manuscript uses these in equations (1)–(2), so an interval-bounded integer polynomial yields a finite operation tree.
- Reference [2], [extended manuscript, arXiv:1212.2889v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.2889v6), Theorem 8.2, printed page 29, with the proof in Section 8: discreteness of Qλ forces λ to be an algebraic integer. The manuscript invokes this at the start of Section 4 with no reality restriction.

Reference [1], Definition 4.1, supplies the strong PV terminology; its Theorem 4.12 supplies the known sufficient direction, and Conjecture 11.1 records the full converse. The submitted paper includes its own norm argument for separation. Campbell's [arXiv:2609.30771v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30771v1), Theorem 2, printed pages 8–10, is credited for the prior totally real case. Draganov's [arXiv:1804.08248v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.08248v2), Section 1, is cited for the classical integer Bernstein construction; Lemma 2 includes the approximation argument used here.
